# Sources and licences

TONE content packs are built automatically from open data. No AI is used. No textbook, exam publisher or website content is copied.

| Pack | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `lexicon-*.json`, `forms.json` | German Wiktionary (de.wiktionary.org), extracted by [wiktextract](https://github.com/tatuylonen/wiktextract) / [kaikki.org](https://kaikki.org/dewiktionary/) | CC BY-SA 4.0 and GFDL |
| frequency ranks / CEFR band estimate | [FrequencyWords](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords) by Hermit Dave (OpenSubtitles 2018) | CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| `sentences-*.json`, `word-sentences.json` | [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org) (field `i` = sentence id: https://tatoeba.org/sentences/show/&lt;id&gt;) | CC BY 2.0 FR |

The derived lexicon packs are shared under **CC BY-SA 4.0**, as the Wiktionary licence requires.
The CEFR band (`lv`) is an estimate based on word frequency. It is not an official Goethe or telc list.
